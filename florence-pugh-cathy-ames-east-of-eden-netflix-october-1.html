Los Angeles, CA — When the cast of Stranger Things gathered for the final red carpet in December 2025, the mood was bittersweet. Most of the ensemble wore solemn black — a sartorial nod to the end of an era. But one figure stood apart: Sadie Sink, who stepped onto the carpet in a crystalline white Prada two‑piece that caught every flashbulb and sent a quiet, radiant message.

Eight months after the series finale, a resurfaced X post (originally shared from the red carpet) has reignited conversation around that night — and around Sink’s meteoric rise. The post, which features Sink beaming in the custom Prada look, contrasts the cast’s collective mourning with her incandescent style choice, framing it as a symbol of renewal. “It wasn’t just a dress,” one fan commented. “It was a statement that she’s ready for what’s next.”

One night, two stories

The Stranger Things finale, which aired in December 2025, drew record viewership and a flood of emotional reactions. The red carpet was a study in contrasts: Millie Bobby Brown, Finn Wolfhard, and Gaten Matarazzo all opted for dark suiting and gowns, while Noah Schnapp wore a midnight blue tuxedo. But Sink, who has portrayed the fierce and loyal Max Mayfield since season two, arrived in a sculptural Prada crop top and high‑waisted skirt, both dripping with silver micro‑sequins that shimmered like starlight.

“I wanted to honor the end of the show, but also celebrate everything it gave me. White felt like a beginning.” — Sadie Sink (backstage, 2025)

In the weeks following the finale, Sink’s team remained quiet about the symbolism, but the X post — which resurfaced in August 2026 — offered a clear narrative: while her co‑stars mourned, Sink was already stepping into the future. The caption reads: “While the Hawkins crew said goodbye in black, Sadie Sink’s Prada moment captured a fresh start — and she’s been running with it ever since.”

From Hawkins to the MCU

That “fresh start” has materialized in spectacular fashion. Since the finale, Sink has landed two major film roles, including the highly anticipated Spider‑Man: Brand New Day, where she plays a mysterious new character opposite Tom Holland’s Peter Parker. While Marvel has kept details under wraps, insiders describe her role as “pivotal” — a far cry from the ’80s‑set horror of Hawkins, Indiana.

A bittersweet goodbye

For the cast of Stranger Things, the December 2025 finale marked the end of a decade‑long journey. Sink, in particular, has spoken warmly about her time on set, calling it “the most formative experience of my life.” Yet she has also made clear that she is eager to explore new characters and genres.

“I’ll always be Max,” she told Interview magazine earlier this year. “But I’m also so many other things. That white Prada outfit was my way of saying: I’m ready for all of them.”
