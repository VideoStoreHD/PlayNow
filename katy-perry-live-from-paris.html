Opening Night: September 2, 2026 – A Global Cinema Event

On Wednesday, September 2, 2026, Katy Perry’s The Lifetimes Tour: Live From Paris will debut in cinemas across 70 countries, including the United States, UK, Italy, Brazil, Mexico, Germany, France, and Australia [citation:3][citation:4]. The release includes exclusive IMAX previews in the US, offering fans the chance to experience the tour’s grandeur on the biggest screens possible.

Following its world premiere at the Tribeca Festival 2026—where Perry walked the red carpet with former Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau—the film has generated significant buzz [citation:12]. Distributed by Trafalgar Releasing, the 132-minute feature includes exclusive behind-the-scenes bonus content that gives fans a deeper look into the making of the tour [citation:1][citation:11].

Captured with 60 Cameras – An Immersive Experience

Filmed across two sold-out nights at Paris’ iconic Accor Arena in November 2025, the concert film transforms one of pop’s most ambitious productions into an immersive cinematic journey [citation:3][citation:5]. Directed by Paul Dugdale, the production uses a groundbreaking 60-camera setup to soar above, around, and through the performance, offering perspectives impossible from the arena floor [citation:4][citation:8].

From Katy flying high above the audience to performing upside down and soaring on a winged butterfly, every moment is crafted for maximum spectacle. The film captures not just the music, but the raw emotion, elaborate choreography, and joyous connection between Perry and her fans [citation:3][citation:9].

Every Anthem, Every Era – The Complete Setlist

The film’s hit-filled setlist spans Katy Perry’s record-breaking career, from early classics to tracks from her 2024 album 143. Songs like “Teenage Dream,” “California Gurls,” “Dark Horse,” “Roar,” and “Firework” sit alongside newer anthems like “Woman’s World,” “Crush,” “Nirvana,” and “Lifetimes” [citation:2].

Notably, “The One That Got Away” has seen a remarkable global resurgence in 2026, reaching the Top 10 of Spotify’s Global Top 50, adding extra poignancy to its inclusion [citation:3]. With over 91 million monthly Spotify listeners and 10 million TikTok followers, Perry’s cultural footprint continues to expand [citation:3].

The Tour Behind the Film: A Global Phenomenon

The Lifetimes Tour (2025) was Katy Perry’s fifth concert tour, in support of her album 143. Spanning 91 shows across 23 countries and five continents, the tour reached more than one million fans worldwide, selling 1,058,950 tickets and generating over $134 million in gross sales [citation:3]. Highlights included six historic performances in China—a rare achievement for Western artists—and charitable contributions to the Firework Foundation and Music Venue Trust [citation:3].

As producer Daniel E. Catullo III noted, “More than a million fans experienced this tour live, and now millions more will have the opportunity to see it like never before on the big screen” [citation:4][citation:3]. The film is described as “Katy’s love letter to her fans”, capturing the essence of a tour that was “larger than life” [citation:3].

Why the Theatrical Experience Matters

In an era of streaming, The Lifetimes Tour: Live From Paris is a reminder of the power of collective viewing. Perry herself emphasized that this film is “really a concert experience at the highest level for the fans,” distinct from her earlier documentary Part of Me [citation:12]. She hopes the film allows fans who couldn’t attend the tour to feel the energy and excitement of the live show from anywhere in the world [citation:9].

With IMAX enhancement, the aerial moments, explosive visuals, and roar of the crowd are amplified to create a shared, immersive experience that home viewing simply cannot replicate [citation:11]. Whether you’re a lifelong KatyCat or a newcomer, this film is a celebration of music, connection, and pop spectacle.
